How to play

♚

Protect your king

Win by checkmating the opposing king: attack it so there is no legal escape.

  1. 1
    White moves first

    Players alternate one move at a time.

  2. 2
    Capture opposing pieces

    Move onto an occupied square when your piece can legally reach it.

  3. 3
    Answer every check

    Move the king, block the attack, or capture the attacking piece.

♜Rook

Any distance across ranks or files.

♝Bishop

Any distance along diagonals.

♛Queen

Moves like a rook and bishop.

♞Knight

Moves in an L shape and can jump.

♚King

One square in any direction.

♟Pawn

Moves forward, captures diagonally.

O-O

Castling

Move the king two squares toward an unmoved rook. The path must be clear and safe.

e.p.

En passant

A pawn may capture a neighboring pawn immediately after it advances two squares.

♟→♛

Promotion

A pawn reaching the farthest rank becomes a queen, rook, bishop, or knight.

#

Checkmate

The checked king has no legal response. The attacking player wins.

½

Draw

Games can draw by stalemate, repetition, insufficient material, or the fifty-move rule.

Time
